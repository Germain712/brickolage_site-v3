7 Simple Steps

A clear, structured process to take your project from idea to handover, with transparency at every stage.

1

Contact Us

Share your project details and goals — we’ll listen, ask the right questions, and schedule a follow-up.

2

Site Visit & Consultation

We visit your site, assess conditions, and discuss options, timelines, and budget in person.

3

Quotation & Agreement

You receive a clear, itemised quotation and scope of work — once agreed, we lock in the plan.

4

Foundation Work

Excavation, rebar, plumbing, and concrete — we build a strong foundation tailored to the site.

5

Structural Build

Blockwork, columns, slabs, and framing — your structure takes shape with solid, reliable workmanship.

6

Finishing Touches

Plastering, tiling, painting, fixtures, and finishes — we refine every detail to your specification.

7

Project Handover

Final walkthrough, snag list, and handover — you receive a completed project ready to live in or use.
